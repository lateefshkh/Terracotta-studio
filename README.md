# Terracotta Brand Guide

Build a luxury custom home builder / construction company website. This is a premium, editorial-feeling marketing site — think high-end architecture firm meets boutique real estate brand. Warm, earthy, sophisticated — never corporate or generic.

=== BRAND & AESTHETIC ===

Company: "Terracotta Builders" — luxury custom home builder, Danville, CA / Bay Area.

Overall mood: warm terracotta/clay tones against deep espresso brown and soft cream. Feels like golden-hour architectural photography — confident, understated, high craftsmanship.

COLOR PALETTE:

- Deep espresso brown (near-black): #2B211B — used for header nav bg, footer bg, dark section backgrounds

- Terracotta/burnt clay (primary accent): #B5673D — used for CTA buttons, icons, section labels, testimonial band

- Warm off-white / cream (background): #FAF6F1 — main content background

- Soft beige (secondary bg): #F0E9E0 — alternating section background

- Charcoal text: #2B211B for headings, #6B6459 muted brown-gray for body copy

- White for text on dark/terracotta backgrounds

TYPOGRAPHY:

- Headings: elegant serif (e.g. "Fraunces", "Playfair Display", or "Canela") — large, generous line-height, used for all H1/H2 ("Homes Crafted for Generations", "Comprehensive Building Services, Tailored to You.")

- Body/UI text: clean humanist sans-serif (e.g. "Inter" or "Söhne") — small, wide letter-spacing for eyebrow/label text (all-caps, tracked out, terracotta color, e.g. "LUXURY CUSTOM HOME BUILDER", "WHAT WE DO", "FEATURED PROJECTS")

- Nav links: small, uppercase, tracked-out sans-serif

=== SITE STRUCTURE (single long-scroll homepage) ===

1. STICKY HEADER (dark espresso bg, fixed on scroll)

   - Left: logo mark (simple line-drawn house/roof icon) + "TERRACOTTA BUILDERS" wordmark, small tagline "BUILDERS" beneath in tracked caps

   - Center/right nav: HOME, ABOUT, SERVICES, PORTFOLIO, OUR PROCESS, JOURNAL, CONTACT (uppercase, small, cream text, active link underlined in terracotta)

   - Right: solid terracotta pill button "LET'S TALK"

2. HERO SECTION (full-bleed photo, dark overlay gradient bottom-left to transparent top-right)

   - Full-width nighttime/dusk photo of a luxury custom home exterior, warm interior lighting glowing through windows, landscaped entry

   - Overlaid text bottom-left: small tracked eyebrow "LUXURY CUSTOM HOME BUILDER" in terracotta, then huge serif H1 "Homes Crafted for Generations" in white across two lines

   - Supporting paragraph (light gray/white, ~3 lines) below headline

   - Two CTA buttons side by side: solid terracotta "VIEW OUR WORK" + outlined/ghost white "SCHEDULE A CONSULTATION"

3. SERVICES SECTION (cream background)

   - Left column: small terracotta eyebrow "WHAT WE DO", large serif H2 "Comprehensive Building Services, Tailored to You." with a short terracotta underline rule beneath

   - Right: 4-column grid of service cards (white cards, soft shadow, generous padding), each with:

     - Line-icon in terracotta circle/square outline at top (house, crossed tools, sofa/chair, clipboard)

     - Serif card title: Custom Home Building / Design + Build / Whole Home Renovations / Project Management

     - Short sans-serif description (2 lines)

     - "LEARN MORE →" small tracked link in terracotta

4. FEATURED PROJECTS SECTION (soft beige background)

   - Left: small eyebrow "FEATURED PROJECTS", serif H2 "Inspired Spaces. Built Beautifully." and a terracotta outlined button "VIEW ALL PROJECTS"

   - Right: 3-across horizontal image gallery of real project photos (exterior twilight shot, kitchen interior, modern hillside home), each captioned below with:

     - Project name (serif, e.g. "Vista Encantada")

     - Location in small tracked caps gray text

     - "VIEW PROJECT →" terracotta link

5. STORY / ABOUT SECTION (cream background, split layout)

   - Left: photo of the founder in a warm, softly lit study/office setting, seated, looking at camera, sweater, confident-but-approachable

   - Right: small eyebrow "OUR STORY", large serif H2 "Built on Integrity. Focused on Excellence." (two lines, terracotta could highlight one phrase), 2 short paragraphs of body copy, and a signature graphic + founder name/title below ("James Morgan, Founder")

6. PROCESS SECTION (cream/white background)

   - Left: eyebrow "OUR PROCESS", serif H2 "A Thoughtful Process. From Start to Finish." + outlined button "HOW WE WORK"

   - Right: horizontal 4-step timeline connected by a thin dotted line, each step a terracotta circular icon (numbered 01–04) with icon (person, pencil/tools, hammer, house), bold label (DISCOVER / DESIGN / BUILD / DELIVER), and one-line description beneath each

7. TESTIMONIAL BAND (full-width solid terracotta background, white text)

   - Large decorative quote mark on the left

   - Italic/serif testimonial quote text, medium size, 2 lines

   - Right-aligned: circular client headshot, name + location (e.g. "Sarah & Michael Thompson, Lafayette, California")

   - Small left/right arrow carousel controls + dot pagination at bottom

8. CONTACT/CTA SECTION (cream background, split layout)

   - Left: styled interior product photo (vase, books, styled console table)

   - Right: serif H2 "Let's Build Something Extraordinary Together.", solid terracotta button "SCHEDULE A CONSULTATION", and a small contact info block below with icons: phone number, email, address, service area line

9. FOOTER (dark espresso background, white/cream text, 4-5 columns)

   - Col 1: logo mark + wordmark, short tagline, social icons (Instagram, houzz/pinterest style icons) in outlined circles

   - Col 2: "COMPANY" — About, Our Process, Careers,
